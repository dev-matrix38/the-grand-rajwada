About The Grand Rajwada

The Grand Rajwada is one of the premier venues, standing out as the top luxury wedding venue in Delhi NCR. With 25+ years of combined experience in hosting ultra-luxury weddings in Delhi NCR, we know how to make your meaningful occasion an unforgettable experience. Situated within Ambience Island, this place is the best blend of royal grandeur with understated modern elegance.

The Grand Rajwada provides both a large and a beautiful space for your wedding at a destination that is safe and easy for your guests to get to. Our in-house wedding specialists have executed over 2000+ weddings at The Grand Rajwada, including celebrity and high-profile weddings. We understand bespoke decor, world class hospitality, and food of the highest culinary standards.

From its luxurious ambiance to the immaculate décor, every nook and corner at The Grand Rajwada speaks of class and good taste. The picturesque approach leading up to The Grand Rajwada is ideal for newlyweds who wish to celebrate in a venue that is truly majestic.

FAQS

Answers to Your Questions

Planning starts with the right information. If something is not covered here, our events team is one call away at +91 9570151834.

1. Is the Décor included in the rental and what would it cost for additional décor? +
Yes, our standard luxury signature décor package (including floral entrance, stage backdrop, intelligent lighting, and dining setup) is included in the rental. Custom theme enhancements can be tailored to your requirements with our in-house design team.
2. Do you provide your catering or outside? +
We offer bespoke in-house catering with world-class multi-cuisine chefs, separate pure vegetarian and non-vegetarian kitchens, and live gourmet counters. We also accommodate empaneled outside caterers upon request.
3. Does the rental for The Grand Rajwada also include Catering? +
We provide both all-inclusive packages (Venue + Décor + Gourmet Catering) as well as venue-only rental options, giving you complete flexibility for your dream celebration.
4. What is the car parking capacity and valet service? +
The Grand Rajwada provides dedicated safe parking for over 500+ cars with complimentary professional valet drivers and full CCTV surveillance.
